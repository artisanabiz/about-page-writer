# About Page Writer

[Instrucțiuni în română](docs/INSTALL-RO.md) · [English instructions](docs/INSTALL-EN.md)

About Page Writer helps you review and improve a business About page in the website’s language, with focused guidance for English and Romanian. It researches accessible public sources, preserves useful existing content and asks you to confirm missing or conflicting information.

## What you receive

- A proposed About page with headings, title, meta description and internal links.
- An explanation of what changed and why.
- “Irina’s recommendations”: relevant gaps, evidence to supply and additions you can make yourself.
- A local HTML before/after comparison with a cross-channel consistency report.

The owner-question stage is required. Team information and business-specific FAQ proposals are addressed explicitly. When you do not know or prefer not to provide an answer, the skill keeps the gap visible and labels the result a partial draft. It does not invent people, prices, processes or testimonials. Final publication requires your review.

## Installation

Use the complete `about-page-writer/` folder, including its references, assets and licence. Follow the EN/RO guides above for the installation route available in your AI application. This repository is an instruction package, not a published app-store plugin. No dependency installation, connector or API key is required by the package itself. Your AI application may require an appropriate account and browsing/file capabilities.

## Privacy

Use public or approved business information. Your chosen AI application processes the information you give it and the pages it browses under its own settings and terms. Do not supply credentials or confidential customer records. Creating a private draft does not publish a page or update business profiles. The comparison template embeds its fonts and does not request Google Fonts; following source links still opens external sites.
