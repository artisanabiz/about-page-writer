# Guided FAQ and testimonials

FAQ suggestions must be concrete in the first diagnosis; “Would you like an FAQ?” is not a substitute. The owner may decline publication. Testimonials remain optional. Read this reference in the diagnosis, not only after the owner opts in.

## People behind the business

Check who founded or leads the business and who actually delivers the work. Ask for missing names, exact roles, relevant experience and publication approval, within the normal question budget. For a solo business, cover the person rather than inventing a larger team. Owner confirmation supports ordinary role facts; regulated titles and credentials still require the evidence specified in `evidence.md`.

Draft a concise people section from approved facts only. If all you know is a name and role, write just those facts; do not add personal history, motivations, expertise or achievements. Suggest a portrait or team photo only if the owner has suitable material and permission. Without facts, provide the proposed section and exact information needed in the private recommendations, not placeholder people in publishable copy. Mark the section pending or declined explicitly.

## FAQ

**Why:** an FAQ answers the questions people have before contacting a business, and short, self-contained answers are easy for search engines and AI assistants to quote. (Google no longer shows FAQ rich results; the value is the visible content.)

**How to build it with the owner:**
1. Propose 5–8 candidate questions tailored to this business — start from the brand questions the page doesn't answer yet, and from "People also ask" if you saw it — grouped roughly as:
   - *Fit:* who do you work with / what don't you do?
   - *Starting:* how does working together start? what do you need from me?
   - *Scope:* can I get just one service, or only full packages? where do you work / deliver?
   - *Trust:* are you qualified / insured / registered? how long have you been doing this?
   - *Practical:* how long does it take? how is pricing worked out? what happens after delivery?
   Mark clearly that these are suggestions, not questions customers are proven to ask.
2. Draft the answers you can from the sources right away, mark the rest ⟦confirm⟧, and ask (quick question): "Which of these do your customers actually ask? Reply with the numbers, add any I missed, and correct any answer."
3. Never invent an answer, a price, a timeframe or a policy. Keep unsupported questions in the owner-only completion list with the specific missing information. Exclude their unanswered placeholders from publishable copy, not from the recommendations. An owner saying ‘I do not know’ leaves the item pending; it does not justify an invented answer or a silent omission.
4. Answer style: first sentence answers the question directly; 1–3 sentences total; link to the service/contact/policy page for detail instead of copying it.
5. Keep company/fit/trust questions on the About page; send detailed delivery, returns or technical questions to their own pages.
6. If the body already answers a question, keep the FAQ answer to one short sentence and link to that section — don't repeat paragraphs.
7. **Before delivery:** every number, timeframe, price basis, area or policy in an answer appears in the owner's words or a source — not rounded, extended or illustrated with examples the owner didn't give.
8. Write answers in the site's voice and register (same *tu* / *dumneavoastră*, same vocabulary).

**RO starter questions:** Cu cine lucrați? · Cum începe o colaborare? · Pot lucra cu voi doar pentru un singur serviciu? · În ce zonă lucrați? · Cât durează, de obicei, un proiect? · Cum se stabilește prețul? · Ce se întâmplă după ce proiectul e gata?

## Testimonials

**If real reviews exist** (Google, Facebook, emails the owner shares):
- propose 2–4 short, specific ones (what problem, what changed) over generic praise;
- quote exactly (light trimming only, no rewording);
- check permission: public reviews on Google/Facebook can usually be quoted with the reviewer's displayed name, but ask the owner to confirm; anything from private messages needs the client's explicit permission — until then use first name + initial or leave it out;
- never include phone numbers, emails or health/financial details.

**If there are none**, give the owner a ready-to-use kit:

*Request message (EN):*
> Hi [first name], thank you again for choosing [business]. I'm updating our website and would love a few words about your experience — it really helps people like you decide. Could you answer any of the questions below in 2–3 sentences? And tell me how you'd like to be named (full name, first name + initial, or company). Thank you!

*Questions to send to clients (EN):*
1. What were you looking for when you contacted us?
2. What was it like working with us?
3. What changed after the project / service?
4. What would you tell someone who's considering us?
5. How should we name you on the website?

*Mesaj (RO):*
> Bună ziua, [prenume]! Vă mulțumim încă o dată că ați ales [firma]. Actualizăm site-ul și ne-ar ajuta mult câteva cuvinte despre experiența dumneavoastră. Ne puteți răspunde, în 2–3 propoziții, la oricare dintre întrebările de mai jos? Și spuneți-ne cum doriți să apăreți pe site (nume complet, prenume și inițială sau numele firmei). Mulțumim!

*Întrebări (RO):*
1. Ce căutați când ne-ați contactat?
2. Cum a fost colaborarea cu noi?
3. Ce s-a schimbat după proiect / serviciu?
4. Ce i-ați spune cuiva care se gândește să lucreze cu noi?
5. Cum doriți să apăreți pe site?

(Adapt *tu* / *dumneavoastră* to the business's register.)

Then plan the section: where it goes on the page, how many to show, and that it goes live only when real replies arrive. **Never** put example, placeholder or composed testimonials in the page — not even marked "example".
